# External reference points considered | Not a conformance statement

These are examples of professional material relevant to interpreting ACR's source and future implementation. The listed organizations have not endorsed or certified ACR, and their logos are not used. Published material is distinguished from the full text of any proprietary standard that has not been audited.

- **Protocol and requirements terminology:** IETF RFC 2119 and RFC 8174 (BCP 14), where expressly adopted by Core. [RFC Editor: BCP 14](https://www.rfc-editor.org/info/bcp14)
- **Software release integrity:** NIST SP 800-218, SSDF 1.1, PS.2; release provenance and test design. [NIST: SP 800-218](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/218/final)
- **Software supply-chain trust:** SLSA 1.2 artifact-verification expectations. [SLSA: verifying artifacts](https://slsa.dev/spec/v1.2/verifying-artifacts)
- **Agent security and tool authorization:** OWASP AI Agent Security Cheat Sheet. [OWASP: agent security](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/AI_Agent_Security_Cheat_Sheet.html)
- **Instruction precedence in assistants:** Wallace and colleagues (2024), *The Instruction Hierarchy*; privileged instructions over user messages over third-party content, with over-refusal as a reported cost. [arXiv:2404.13208](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.13208)
- **Multi-agent failure patterns:** Cemri and colleagues (2025), *Why Do Multi-Agent LLM Systems Fail?* [arXiv:2503.13657](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.13657)
- **Interruption:** Orseau and Armstrong (2016), *Safely Interruptible Agents*. [PMLR](https://proceedings.mlr.press/r14/orseau16a.html)
- **Tool integration:** Model Context Protocol specification, revision 2026-07-28, and earlier revisions for the initialize handshake. [Specification](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2026-07-28/server/tools)
- **Audit procedures:** ISO 19011:2026 publisher overview; guidance, not certification. [ISO: publisher](https://www.iso.org/)
- **Mathematical verification:** Trusted-kernel and proof-reconstruction practices; mathematical correctness must be assessed on exact hypotheses and proofs rather than on format.
- **Scientific research:** National Academies, *Reproducibility and Replicability in Science* (2019), including the distinction between re-running computation and using new evidence. [National Academies: report](https://nap.nationalacademies.org/catalog/25303/reproducibility-and-replicability-in-science)
- **Statistical conduct:** American Statistical Association, *Ethical Guidelines for Statistical Practice* (2022); assumptions, fitness, limitations and correction. [ASA: ethical guidelines](https://www.amstat.org/your-career/ethical-guidelines-for-statistical-practice)
- **Engineering safety:** Domain-specific safety cases, independent system hazards and qualified protective controls; ACR conformance is not a substitute for applicable engineering standards.
- **Cryptography:** NIST entropy-source and algorithm/security standards when relevant; statistical randomness batteries alone cannot establish unpredictability.
- **Quality Assurance:** IEEE 730:2026 and ISO 9001:2026 publisher guidance as context-dependent comparisons, not evidence of ACR conformity.

Do not put standards-compliance claims or institutional logos on module covers without an independently supported, legally appropriate basis. These references do not establish certification or endorsement.
