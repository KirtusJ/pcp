### Do not turn the card into a ceremony

Populate facts already established by trustworthy context. Leave unavailable material facts explicitly unresolved. A missing prerequisite holds only the dependent act. An Entry Card does not authorize tools, create a Review, select modules by itself, establish LLM calibration, or certify a result. The same meanings may be carried in an existing task record without using this card.
