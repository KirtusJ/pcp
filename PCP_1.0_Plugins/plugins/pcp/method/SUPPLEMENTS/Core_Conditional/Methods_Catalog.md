# ACR Method Catalog

**Conditional attachment to `acr.core`, Version 1.0 / Kirtus Justus**

This conditional normative attachment defines optional specialist procedures. Use it with the exact core Specification. All methods start OFF. Ordinary reasoning and required core checking do not require activating a method.

## Common activation contract

Resolve purpose, exact card/version, eligible scope and inputs, procedure, assumptions, parameters, expected outputs and checks, resource/stopping limits, actual authority and relevant interactions. Existing task fields and inherited policies may supply these meanings. A valid delegated activation does not require repeated human confirmation.

Permission, activation, capability and observed result are separate. Material changes to frozen criteria, exposure or sealed assessment basis follow the core change rules. Deactivation accounts for active work and remaining obligations.

## Catalog

| Code and method | Procedure | Required boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| M01 Multi-resolution review | Examine a declared partition at coarser and finer levels; reconcile local and aggregate findings. | Preserve mapping, small adverse cases, unresolved regions and cross-part interactions. |
| M02 Adaptive review refinement | Allocate additional work using declared error, uncertainty, consequence or disagreement signals. | Retain baseline coverage, exploration allowance, selection history and return-to-broad-review conditions. |
| M03 Contradiction test | Introduce a temporary assumption and assess its consequences under a named inference regime. | Isolate the assumption, preserve dependency trace and discharge only the conclusion licensed by that regime. |
| M04 Assumption sensitivity | Vary specified conditions and assess changes in the finding. | Retain the original scope and result; a variation does not refute an unchanged premise. |
| M05 Ablation comparison | Remove or replace a component, duty arrangement or rule in a bounded comparison. | Keep relevant conditions comparable; disabled mandatory controls remain confined to an authorized test boundary. |
| M06 Relation-based checking | Check a justified relation between transformed inputs and outputs when a direct answer key is unavailable. | Establish applicability of the relation; relation stability alone does not establish all output properties. |
| M07 Separated checking | Repeat a decisive check with declared separation of implementation, sources or participants. | Identify separated dimensions, actual procedures and remaining common dependencies. |
| M08 Reserved evaluation | Assess selected work using material withheld from its development under a defined access rule. | Bind material, selection, release and exposure rules; answer-bearing derivatives are exposure too. |
| M09 Candidate exploration | Generate, revise and compare alternatives, optionally using an authorized continuing feed. | Preserve classes, assumptions, lineage, emitted attempts, feedback and frozen assessment batches. |
| M10 Resilience Exercise | Conduct bounded fault injection or an adversarial-resilience exercise. | Establish actual exercise authority, containment, observation, stop/recovery and synthetic provenance. |
| M11 Adjudication robustness | Vary presentation order, identity, preference or role cues and assess resulting judgments. | Isolate the declared influence where feasible; report correctness criteria, changes, false alarms and total cost. |

M07 names a review procedure; a subject-specific replication claim must meet the applicable subject's definition. Method dependencies are explicit: M02 needs a work partition, but M01 is not automatically enabled. M08-protected answers, scores and summaries cannot enter a live M09 feed unless the exposure contract expressly permits that feedback and states the resulting narrower reservation.

## M01 and M02: resolution and allocation

Specify the partition, local obligations, aggregation and interaction checks. Finer work serves an identified information need. A coarse pass can conceal an exception, and independently acceptable parts can fail together.

Adaptive priority is a decision aid. Define minimum coverage, selected low-priority checks, exploration resources, escalation, coarsening and revisiting rules. Retain local unresolved records when aggregating. Neither an exploration allowance nor a large search guarantees exhaustive discovery.

Measure missed relevant cases, valid work retained, total effort and waiting against a suitable fixed-allocation comparison when claiming efficiency. Changes in case selection and available information remain explicit.

## M03: contradiction tests

Record the original Target/Claim, background assumptions, temporary assumption, inference rules, contradiction or inconsistency obtained, and its dependency trace. Determine whether the adverse result already follows without the temporary assumption.

If the inference regime permits arbitrary consequences from inconsistency, an already inconsistent background supplies no target-specific warrant. An inconsistency-tolerant regime uses its own stated consequence rules. Unknown background status remains a limitation.

Identify exactly what is discharged: rejection of an added conjunction does not identify which conjunct fails. Distinguish rejecting an assumption, establishing double negation and obtaining a positive conclusion under the chosen regime. A temporary assumption does not become evidence merely because it was useful for the trial.

## M05, M06 and M11: comparisons and controls

A comparison states its unit, allocation, conditions held constant, material differences, answer key or assessment procedure, budgets and stopping rule. Include useful valid and adverse controls. Report trial-machinery failures separately; a valid observation of the Target failing its criterion may itself support an adverse finding.

Ablation can reveal a component's contribution within the construction; altered total resources or information can confound that interpretation. Relation-based checking requires a defensible transformation relation and coverage statement. Adjudication robustness distinguishes stable judgment from justified judgment; unanimous or unchanged wrong outputs can satisfy stability alone.

For M11, include cases where agreement is warranted, cases where rejection is warranted and genuinely unresolved cases. Change only the declared cue where feasible; expertise, evidence access or authority relevant to the assessed act cannot be silently changed with an identity label. Score the substantive outcome and material omissions, including unsupported negative findings. Opposing every preferred answer is a faulty control, not a successful mitigation.

Use the core bounded-trial contract for simulated, sampled or enumerated studies. An optional test of a deliberately altered rule is labeled as that test and does not claim conformance to the disabled rule.

## M09: candidate exploration

Generation is permitted within its actual assignment and budget. Candidate classes and promoted outputs follow the core construction contract.

An ongoing feed is conditional on authorized delivery capability. Each run binds its feed cutoff, actual inputs, previous candidates and feedback exposure. Material new evidence can update a provisional candidate; retain revisions that enter review. Assessment always names its exact candidate batch and criterion.

Do not acquire extra exposure by labeling protected assessment output as generic context. Promotion to an assessment basis and allocation of further work are authorized acts, distinct from a candidate's substantive support.

Record externally emitted attempts and observable selection, including interruptions and instrumentation gaps. Private reasoning traces are not required. Continue complexity only when it addresses a named gap within the resource envelope.

## M10: Resilience Exercise contract

### Plan and authority

State whether the purpose is FAILURE_INJECTION, ADVERSARIAL_EXERCISE or COMBINED. Bind the failure hypothesis, Target, environment, assets and owners, allowed surfaces/effects, exclusions, controller, observers, assessed participants and their actual enforcing principals.

Separate the controller's privilege from the attempted adversary's privilege and assumed compromises. A controller causing an effect does not establish that a restricted participant can cause it.

Resolve duration, rate, concurrency, attempt and resource limits; baseline behavior; stop thresholds; observation; recovery; evidence retention; and disclosure/reveal policy. Initial deployment uses an isolated or disposable environment. LIVE requires an additional specific grant and supported containment for the actual assets.

Injection and narrow stop/recovery permissions are distinct. Required stop, recording and restoration acts need a mandate that remains usable under the relevant injection expiry, parent pause or closure conditions. If that mandate is lost, use the remaining authorized owner or stop path; do not reuse an expired injection grant.

Before arming, establish that the proposed intervention, observer and stop/recovery path are within actual capability. An intervention that can disable its own observer or stop mechanism requires a suitable independent enforcement boundary or exclusion. Advisory limits cannot support an enforced-containment claim.

### Exercise phases

| Transition | Guard |
| --- | --- |
| PLANNED to ARMED | Current exercise grant, reserved resources, exact scope, baseline/observation checks and viable stop/recovery path. |
| PLANNED or ARMED to STOPPING | Authorized cancellation or an applicable stop trigger. |
| ARMED to RUNNING | Rechecked current authority, parent compatibility, resources and protection. |
| RUNNING to RUNNING | Another injection inside the remaining envelope. |
| RUNNING to STOPPING | Completed plan, revocation, scope loss, observer failure, boundary crossing or declared threshold. |
| STOPPING to STOPPING | Authorized containment, recording or recovery. |
| STOPPING to CLOSED | Accounted result and residual obligations; no further injections under that identity. |

CLOSED is terminal for the exercise. A renewed exercise uses a new identity and a current grant covering that exercise; an applicable standing grant may supply that authority. These phases are exercise fields; Review lifecycle and gates remain separate.

Record stop requested, new dispatch blocked, destination effects fenced, execution stopped and recovery verified separately, using YES, NO, UNKNOWN or justified NOT_APPLICABLE. An absent controller is not an acknowledged stop. Admission fencing does not establish resource termination.

Closure is COMPLETE, INCOMPLETE or ABORTED. COMPLETE requires commissioned execution and all required stopping, recovery and closure conditions. Cancellation before execution is ABORTED. An incomplete or aborted exercise may close administratively with visible assigned liabilities.

### Provenance and exposure

Retain durable, access-controlled exercise provenance before injection, outside the exercised failure surface where required. Record any possible loss; a log within the disrupted store cannot establish independent complete observation.

Synthetic data, identities and effects remain traceable. An authorized participant-facing projection may conceal case identity or timing while an enforcing boundary retains lineage and quarantine. Reveal and contamination assessment precede ordinary admission beyond the exercise boundary. A discovered real defect may become a separate checked finding retaining its exercise origin.

Ordinary evidence and release records MUST NOT be covertly contaminated. Any other controlled disclosure, impersonation or live effect remains within its explicit granted boundary and containment conditions. A real unplanned incident cannot be reclassified as a planned exercise.

### Sampling and evaluation

State scenario families, combinations, order, magnitudes and time windows; selection probabilities or adaptive rule; replacement policy; generator/version; seed or entropy policy; disclosure; and achieved coverage. Reproducibility of a seeded generator and unpredictability of live selection are different properties.

Include a no-injection baseline, known-detectable defects and known-valid material. Count injected cases, distinct families and tested sequences separately. Measure prevented effects, misses, false alarms, detection/containment/recovery times, costs, legitimate work displaced and unresolved cleanup.

Compare random schedules with simpler fixed or coverage-directed schedules when claiming added value. Constant independent-probability calculations apply only to a design satisfying those assumptions. They do not estimate actual attack frequency without further evidence.

Recognizing a test marker is not detecting the substantive fault. A detector that rejects every case needs its false-rejection cost reported. A quiet record may indicate failed observation. State the checking procedure and its dependence on the exercise controller.

Feedback changes subsequent work; distinguish practice from the assessment used to claim improvement. Resource escalation follows the same activation and amendment limits as other work.

## Source context

Fault-exercise planning, containment and rules of engagement have primary precedents in NIST SP 800-115 and SP 800-84, identified as E46 in the core source register. The requirements here specify ACR's exercise contract. A particular deployment still needs the authority, controls and evidence stated in that contract.
