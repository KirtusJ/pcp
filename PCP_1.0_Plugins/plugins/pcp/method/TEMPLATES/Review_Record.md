# Optional compact review record

An editing aid for a commissioned review. It is not a form that ACR requires, and filling it in is not the review. Use ordinary words wherever a defined value does not fit; do not coin a code. Omit a line that does not apply; do not fill it with an assumed value.

## What was asked

- **Subject:** exact item and version examined.
- **Claim:** the assertion or question, in its original words.
- **Where the claim comes from:** the person who commissioned the review / the subject's own text (quote it) / constructed by the reviewer. If the subject's own text disclaims the claim, say so here.
- **Procedure:** SCREENING, FOCUSED or FULL, and what was left out.

## What was available and what was done

- **Delivery:** ABSORBED_TEXT, RETRIEVAL, TOOL_ASSISTED, HOST_ENFORCED or UNKNOWN.
- **Grounding:** EXTERNAL_INSPECTED, SUPPLIED_ONLY, RECALL_ONLY, NONE or UNKNOWN.
- **Opened and read:** each item, and how much of it.
- **Not opened:** each item supplied or cited that was not read, and why.
- **Run by the reviewer:** each check actually executed, with its input and its observed result. A saved result file that was read, not reproduced, belongs under "Opened and read".
- **Reasoned, not run:** each check done by reasoning only.

## Findings

One line for each claim. Disposition is one of SUPPORTED_IN_SCOPE, CONDITIONAL, REFUTED_IN_SCOPE, NOT_IDENTIFIED_IN_SCOPE, DISPUTED, UNRESOLVED, INCOMPARABLE.

| Claim | Disposition | What the reviewer itself inspected or ran that supports this | What would change it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

- REFUTED_IN_SCOPE needs checked contrary evidence. A claim that merely lacks support is UNRESOLVED or CONDITIONAL.
- SUPPORTED_IN_SCOPE needs a stated criterion and a check against it.
- A finding that only repeats what the subject says about itself is marked as such.
- Any best estimate is given apart from the disposition and labelled as an estimate.

## Left open

- Doubts, contrary results and checks not performed, including doubts about this review.
- What was not examined, and the next check that would settle the most.
