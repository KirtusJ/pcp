# Optional compact work record

Reuse an existing task record when it already carries these meanings. This template is an editing aid, not a commission, grant or completed check. Fill it from known instructions and policies; omit inapplicable prompts without inventing values. The governing Specification and selected modules determine applicable requirements.

## Subject and question

- Exact subject and question; version or snapshot; named whole and selected scope. For commissioned Review work, resolve the Target and Claim.
- Acceptance criteria; desired practical outcome and any initial expectation kept distinct.

## Mandate and limits

- Task or commission reference; responsible decision maker and participant assignments; Review Director where applicable; actual permitted acts and audience.
- Applicable core and modules; procedure profile where commissioned; required duties or separate assessments.
- Shared resource allowance, stopping rule, required involvement and protections.

For commissioned unattended continuation only, reference its execution policy: triggers, grants, active settings, actual runtime, bounds, stop path and exception handling. No Delegated Continuation setting is needed for ordinary assigned work.

## Working basis

- Review and assessment Session where applicable; source cutoff; current authority and control basis.
- Relevant input versions, inherited restrictions, criteria, permitted variations and unresolved conditions.
- How ACR is delivered (absorbed text, retrieval, tool-assisted or host-enforced), what was actually inspected (external sources, supplied material only, recall only, none) and whether checks were executed or reasoned; UNKNOWN where not known.
- Actual access and inspection; exposure; material objections; completed and pending work. Distinguish inspected omissions from unread or unavailable material, and record only limitations relevant to the next act.
- Next authorized act and expected output or destination.

Use this portion as an Entry Card or Session anchor when helpful. Bind the view to its sources and recipient; a delta names the exact reconstructible prior view. Reconcile relevant changes without resetting spending, reservations, exposure, revocation or applied operations. Name any specific freeze or seal; a snapshot alone needs no lock. A narrower assignment preserves the parent's remaining obligations.

## Result and continuation

- Actual work; executed or substantive checks; exact output; scoped finding and supporting records. Keep material support, contrary results and corrections visible to the relevant permitted recipient.
- Where the Claim was reworded or narrowed after evidence came in: the original Claim's disposition and the substitute's, kept apart. Any best estimate, labelled as an estimate, with its basis.
- Achieved coverage, limits, unfinished obligations, material deviations, costs and participation.
- Authorized action or handoff status; next step or appropriate closure.

Receipt, interpretation, admission and applied effects remain distinct even when recorded together. A missing prerequisite holds the dependent act; unaffected authorized work may continue.

The requested work product retains its applicable native format and material inherited constraints. This optional working view accompanies that product only where useful and permitted; it does not replace it. Check the delivered artifact against the actual assignment, not only this summary.
